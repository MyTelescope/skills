# Pricing - MyTelescope

MyTelescope is a demand intelligence platform. Plans are billed monthly or annually. All plans include access to demand signals from Google, TikTok, YouTube, Amazon, Pinterest, Bing, Etsy, App Store, Play Store, eBay, and Perplexity.

---

## Free

- Price: $0/month
- Topics: Public dashboards only (no custom tracking)
- Features:
  - View public demand dashboards
  - Ask demand questions via AI (natural language Q&A powered by Perplexity API)
  - Access real-time trend graphs from search and conversation data
- Limits: Cannot create custom dashboards or track specific brands/products without upgrading
- Best for: Anyone exploring demand intelligence before committing to a paid plan

---

## Starter

- Price: Custom - contact sales at mytelescope.io
- Topics: Up to 10 topics (brands, products, categories, or themes)
- Features:
  - Custom dashboards with persistent historical data
  - Multi-platform signal aggregation
  - Share of search tracking
  - Month-over-month trend monitoring
  - AI-powered natural language Q&A on your own data
- Best for: Small teams tracking a few brands or products

---

## Professional / Growth

- Price: Custom - contact sales at mytelescope.io
- Topics: Up to 50 topics
- Features:
  - Everything in Starter
  - API access for embedding demand data in external tools
  - Demand forecasting (ARIMA, ETS, Prophet, XGBoost models)
  - Competitive benchmarking and share of search comparison
  - MCP integration for use inside Claude and other AI assistants
  - Real-Demand Pack: monthly CSV/Excel exports
- Best for: Teams that need to integrate MyTelescope data into their own workflows and tools

---

## Enterprise

- Price: Custom - contact sales at mytelescope.io
- Topics: Unlimited (organizations managing multiple brands or markets at scale)
- Features:
  - Everything in Professional
  - White-label options
  - Dedicated onboarding and support
  - Custom data integrations
  - Multi-user team access
  - SLA and audit trail
- Best for: Large organizations with multiple brands, markets, or internal teams

---

## Topic definition

A MyTelescope topic is one tracked subject - a brand, product, category, or theme. One primary search (e.g. "Nike") equals 1 topic. A dashboard comparing Nike vs Adidas, Puma, and Reebok for Mexico equals 1 dashboard and 4 topics.

## MCP access

MCP integration (for use inside Claude and compatible AI assistants) is available on Professional and Enterprise plans.
